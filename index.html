Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 27, 2026

Welcome to Presseddddd! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our app.

TL;DR: We don't collect, store, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your device. Your privacy is our priority.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Presseddddd is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Audio recordings from Scream Mode
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Device information beyond what's required for app functionality
  • Contacts or other personal data from your device

2. Local Data Storage

The app stores certain preferences locally on your device only:

  • User Preferences: Language selection, font size, sound settings
  • Profile Information: Display name and country (stored locally only)
  • App Settings: Theme preferences and customization options

All this data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

3. Permissions

3.1 Microphone Access

Presseddddd may request access to your device's microphone for the Scream Mode feature. This permission is:

  • Optional - you can use the app without granting this permission
  • Used only when you actively use Scream Mode
  • Never used to record, store, or transmit audio
  • Only used for real-time audio level detection to provide visual feedback

3.2 Sound and Haptics

The app uses your device's sound system and vibration motor to provide feedback. No data is collected through these features.

4. Third-Party Services

Presseddddd may use the following third-party services:

4.1 App Stores

  • Google Play Store: When you download or update the app through Google Play, Google's privacy policy applies
  • Apple App Store: When you download or update the app through the App Store, Apple's privacy policy applies

4.2 Expo Framework

The app is built using Expo/React Native. No analytics or tracking services from Expo are enabled in this app.

5. Data Security

Since we don't collect or transmit any personal data, there's no risk of your information being intercepted or compromised during transmission. All your preferences and settings are stored securely on your device using industry-standard local storage mechanisms.

6. Children's Privacy

Presseddddd does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require any personal information and can be used by users of all ages. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app.
